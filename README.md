# Hunch — Enhancing Platform Engagement (Product Case Study) 👻

A product case study exploring how Hunch — an anonymous polling social app —
can boost platform engagement through gamified rewards, mood-personalized
content, and community circles, backed by market sizing, RICE-based feature
prioritization, and a phased go-to-market plan.

## About Hunch

Hunch is a social media app that connects users through anonymous polling,
letting them express opinions and discover like-minded people without
attaching their identity. Co-founded in 2022 by Ish Goel, Nitika Goel, and
Kartic Rakhra, the company has raised $23M from Alpha Wave Global and Hashed,
and reports 1M+ downloads, 220,000 monthly active users, a 3.7/5 rating, and
an average session time of 30 minutes/day.

## Problem Statement

Despite strong engagement (30 min/day average session, 150% YoY growth in
engagement), Hunch's average revenue per download has declined sharply — from
$1.49 in 2017 to roughly $0.54 in 2024, though it has stabilized since 2022.
This case study explores product levers to convert Hunch's already-engaged
user base into deeper platform loyalty and monetization headroom, without
compromising the anonymity that defines the app's core value proposition.

## Approach

1. **Market Sizing** — quantified the opportunity and benchmarked revenue trends
2. **User Personas** — modeled two representative user segments
3. **Feature Prioritization** — scored five candidate features using RICE
4. **MVP Design** — detailed flows for the top three prioritized features
5. **Success Metrics** — defined measurable KPIs per feature
6. **Go-to-Market** — phased rollout and adoption strategy
7. **Risk Analysis** — identified pitfalls with corresponding mitigations

## Market Opportunity

- The global social networking market is projected to grow from **$81.42B (FY2024) to $179.22B (FY2029)**, a **17.09% CAGR**
- India alone has **467 million active social media users** — the second-largest market after China — representing significant headroom for user acquisition
- Revenue-per-download has plateaued since 2022 (~$0.53–0.60), suggesting engagement is stable but monetization strategy needs to evolve independently

## User Personas

- **Rohan (16, Mumbai)** — high schooler active on Instagram/Snapchat, wants to express opinions and discover trends, but is held back by privacy concerns and a lack of platforms built for teen-specific discussion
- **Rahul Yadav (28, Bengaluru)** — a time-constrained marketing manager who wants quick, casual, anonymous interactions during work breaks, but finds mainstream platforms too crowded for meaningful engagement

## Feature Prioritization (RICE)

Five candidate features were scored on Reach × Impact × Confidence ÷ Effort.
The top three, carried into MVP scope, were:

| Rank | Feature | RICE Score |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | HunchIT (gamified rewards) | 89.6 |
| 2 | MoodyHunch (mood-personalized content) | 82.3 |
| 3 | CircleHub (public/private communities) | 81.0 |

Trending topics (56.0) and User Ranking (36.75) scored lower and were
deprioritized out of MVP scope.

## MVP Feature Deep-Dive

**HunchIT** — a points-based reward system where users earn "Hunch points" by
creating polls and commenting, unlocking premium features (e.g., seeing who
voted for what) and tracked, redeemable rewards that refresh monthly. Designed
to incentivize continued participation and increase time spent on the app.

**MoodyHunch** — lets users select or describe their current mood (via
AI-generated prompts or free text), and personalizes the poll feed and
content categories to match. Aims to deepen emotional connection with the app
and improve content relevance beyond generic feeds.

**CircleHub** — splits communities into Public Circles (open, anonymous,
discovery-driven) and Private Circles (invite-only, exclusive, named
members), with search and personalized community recommendations. Intended
to drive both broad community engagement and deeper, higher-trust
interactions within smaller groups.

## Success Metrics

Each MVP feature has dedicated tracking metrics:

- **HunchIT:** daily Hunch points earned, conversion rate to premium
  features, retention of premium users, engagement with progress
  notifications
- **MoodyHunch:** mood selection rate, poll interaction rate by mood, mood
  sharing rate
- **CircleHub:** membership growth rate, public circle interaction rate,
  private circle creation rate, private circle comment interaction rate,
  circle member retention

## Go-to-Market Strategy

A five-step rollout: survey and segment the target audience (engaged teens
and anonymity-seeking adults) → invite-only beta testing with highly engaged
users → targeted social ad campaigns and influencer partnerships emphasizing
anonymity and rewards → user testimonials to build credibility → continuous
monitoring of engagement, participation, and reward-redemption metrics to
guide iteration.

## Possible Pitfalls & Mitigations

| Pitfall | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Insufficient content after mood selection may drive users away | Blend in a smaller share of off-mood content rather than filtering exclusively |
| Inaccurate mood/content recommendations cause frustration | Continuous algorithmic improvement via user feedback and real-time data |
| Free rewards reduce incentive to buy premium | Keep reward-tier features deliberately limited to create appetite for premium |
| Too many CircleHub features at once causes confusion | Guide users through each new feature on first use post-launch |
